(a) Pros: 
    1. More resilient to scope changes in the Project
    2. Constant collaboration with customers

    Cons:
    1. Difficulty in predicting project timelines and costs
    2. High demands on team communication and collaboration


(b) Scrum Master: All scrum related tasks – JIRA tooling, facilitating sprints
    and meetings, standups all done by the Scrum Master

    Product Owner: Sets direction for the team, handles release
    management and stakeholder management. 


(c) 1. Sprint Planning Meeting:
        -> Determine what to complete in the coming sprint
        -> Move stories in sprint scope from backlog to current sprint
        -> Determine story points and assign stories to team
        -> Start sprint

    2. Daily Scrum/Standup Meeting:
        -> Short meeting that happens every day of the sprint
        -> Usually, 10 mins long
        -> Team members discuss work completed, work plan for the day and any blockers
        
    3. Sprint Retrospective Meeting:
        -> Occurs at the end of the sprint
        -> Discuss work complete and move incomplete work to backlog
        -> Close sprin
